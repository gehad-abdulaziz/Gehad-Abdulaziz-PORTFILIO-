AraCheck

Team project · Arabic and English medical RAG assistant

A LangGraph pipeline that asks the LLM first, retrieves evidence when confidence is low, and searches the web only if retrieval is weak too. I worked on the retrieval layer: BAAI/bge-m3 embeddings, Qdrant, cross-encoder reranking and a margin-based confidence score in place of a fixed similarity cutoff. Voice through Whisper and images through a vision-language model feed the same chat, with numbered source citations.

AAA Clinical RAG

Team project · Evidence-first retrieval over aortic aneurysm guidelines

Dense retrieval over four abdominal aortic aneurysm guidelines, 991 chunks in all. A structure-driven chunker lifted P@1, and I evaluated it on three frozen question sets with a controlled ablation. One higher-scoring setup was rejected after the gain traced back to a page-metadata artifact. The index moved to Qdrant and matched local retrieval on all 48 frozen queries.

The generation layer refuses patient-specific and low-confidence questions instead of guessing, and a citation validator checks every answer. A FastAPI backend and Streamlit UI show per-chunk scores and evaluation metrics read live from the frozen artifacts.

Medical ASR v6

Individual project · Multi-specialty clinical speech recognition

faster-whisper large-v3 transcribes the audio and GLiNER pulls out clinical entities without task-specific training. The pipeline detects which of 8 medical specialties it is hearing and picks specialty-aware prompts, then grades each transcript from A to D on ASR and NER confidence for clinical review. The Streamlit app records live, takes uploads and highlights text by confidence.

Multi-Task BiRNN

Individual project · One encoder across SST-2, MRPC, QQP and MNLI

A single shared bidirectional RNN with an ESIM-style soft-alignment layer, trained jointly on sentiment, paraphrase, duplicate-question and inference tasks. Added capacity, soft alignment and more data moved the score up in stages, and label smoothing, gradient clipping and stochastic weight averaging kept training stable. Packaged as a Gradio demo.
